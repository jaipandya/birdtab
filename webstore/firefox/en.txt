DESCRIPTION
Bird wallpapers, bird songs and nature facts on every new tab page in Firefox.

BirdTab replaces your Firefox new tab page with a high-resolution bird photo. Each time you open a tab, you meet a new bird: hear its call, read about the species and see where it lives on a range map. It's a relaxing moment of nature in your day, and an easy way to learn your birds.

**On every new tab**

- A new high-resolution bird wallpaper, from hummingbirds and parrots to eagles and owls
- The bird's real song or call, with volume control and quiet hours (sound is optional)
- Facts about the species: description, conservation status and range map
- Openly licensed photos, each credited to its photographer

**Birds from where you live**

Choose from 250+ countries and regions to see the birds found near you, or pick World for the 500 most common birds on Earth.

**More than a wallpaper**

- Bird identification quiz: test your knowledge and share your results
- Your Birds: every bird you've seen, with favorites and a quiz on your own birds
- Clock and countdown timer
- Search box that uses your default search engine or one you choose
- Shortcuts to your top sites
- Works offline for recently seen birds
- Available in 12 languages: English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, European Portuguese, German, French, Japanese, Polish, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Arabic

**Privacy**

- No ads, no account, no sign-up
- No personal data collected. Anonymous usage statistics and error reports are sent only if you allow them when installing

**Made for**

- Birdwatchers, from beginners to experienced birders
- Nature lovers who want a calm, beautiful new tab
- Students, teachers and anyone curious about wildlife

**What's new**
