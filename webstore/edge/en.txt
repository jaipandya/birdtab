BirdTab is a free Microsoft Edge extension that turns every new tab into a calming nature escape: a stunning bird wallpaper, real bird songs and nature sounds, and fascinating facts about a different bird species every time you open a tab.

BirdTab replaces your Microsoft Edge new tab page with breathtaking bird photography from your own country or from around the world. Every time you open a tab, you'll discover a new bird species - complete with authentic bird calls, nature sounds, and interesting facts. It's like having a window to the natural world right in your browser.

WHY USERS LOVE BIRDTAB

Unlike generic wallpaper extensions, BirdTab is a complete nature experience. Each new tab brings a moment of calm. A beautiful bird photograph, a relaxing bird call, and something new to learn. It's the perfect blend of mindfulness, education, and aesthetic beauty.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is BirdTab free?
Yes. BirdTab is completely free - no paid plans, no ads, and no account needed.

Which birds will I see?
Birds from the country you choose (250+ countries and regions), or the World's 500 most common birds. Every bird comes with a photo, its call, facts, and a link to its eBird page.

Does BirdTab work offline?
Yes. BirdTab keeps birds ready on your device, so a new bird appears even without an internet connection.

Can I turn off the bird sounds?
Yes. Sound is optional - mute it, adjust the volume, or set quiet hours.

Does BirdTab collect my data?
No. BirdTab does not collect personal data or track your browsing.

How do I open the default Microsoft Edge new tab?
Click the Microsoft Edge icon in the top corner of any BirdTab tab.
